❓ HOW TO PLAY ❓
OBJECTIVE
Jump through 5 stages of life journey, avoid obstacles, collect coins and stars to unlock milestones!
🎮 CONTROLS
JUMP: Press SPACE, UP Arrow, or TAP the screen to jump!
DOUBLE JUMP: Press the jump button again while in mid-air for a higher jump!
🪙 COINS
Collect golden coins throughout each stage. Each coin gives you +10 points. Collect multiple coins in a row to build a combo multiplier!
⭐ MILESTONE STARS
Find and touch the milestone stars to unlock special moments from each life stage. There are 5 stars total — one for each stage. Collecting all stars reveals the complete story!
⚠️ OBSTACLES
Avoid obstacles and hazards in your path! Each hit costs you one life. You start with 100 lives in this sample version. Game over when lives reach 0.
📊 PROGRESSION
There are 5 stages to complete, each representing a chapter of life. Clear all obstacles and reach the end of each stage to progress. Can you beat all 5?
📋 About this game
WHAT THIS IS
This is an interactive game that tells the story of who I am — Ian Jay Kyle D. Tipanero. Each of the 5 stages represents a real chapter of my life, and every milestone star you collect reveals a true personal achievement. It's not just a game; it's my personal brand in interactive form.
"Instead of a pesonal product branding, I built something a little different — a game that tells you who I am. Each stage is a real chapter of my life. Let me walk you through it."

🏠 Stage 1 – Childhood Life: "I grew up in Camarines Sur. The province taught me to be resourceful and curious — both skills I still use in IT today."

📚 Stage 2 – High School Life: "This is where I first touched technology. Joining the tech club showed me that building things digitally was what I wanted to do."

💻 Stage 3 – College at CSPC: "I'm currently a 4th-year IT student. I've learned HTML, CSS, JS, React, MySQL, Git, and UI/UX design — basically full-stack."

🎨 Stage 4 – Hobbies & Skills: "Beyond code I also do graphic design, photography, playing instrument, and exploring new places. My creative hobbies make my technical work stand out visually."

🚀 Stage 5 – Internship & Future: "Right now I'm applying everything as an IT intern. I also take freelance design and web commissions. My goal is to be a full creative technologist."
